## 📌 Note on the Pitch Video
During the recording of our video pitch, the audio cut off in the final seconds just as we began explaining our flagship **EQ Read & Negotiation** feature. While the audio was lost, the feature is fully functional in the live app. Contracty doesn't just flag risks; it explains the negotiating power dynamic behind the wording and provides copy-pasteable scripts to help you push back.

## 🚀 Future Roadmap
We plan to evolve Contracty by replacing hardcoded regex with live **IBM Granite API** integration for dynamic clause analysis, adding an interactive **Negotiation Simulator**, and implementing **jurisdiction-aware legal warnings**. Ultimately, we aim to become the "Grammarly for Contracts"—empowering small businesses to sign with confidence.
